Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: March 7, 2026  ·  Nolvek, Atlanta, GA

Plain English summary: We only collect information you deliberately give us (like your name and email when you fill out our contact form). We don't sell your data. We don't run advertising trackers on this site. We'll only use your information to contact you about the services you asked about.

1. Who We Are

Nolvek ("we," "us," or "our") is a digital services company based in Atlanta, Georgia. We build websites, manage advertising campaigns, develop AI automation systems, and create custom software for local businesses. You can reach us at:

  • Email: kevlongalloway1999@icloud.com
  • Location: Atlanta, Georgia, United States
  • Website: nolvek-dev.onrender.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to respond to your inquiry and deliver our services. This falls into two categories:

Information you provide directly

When you submit the contact form on our website, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Which service you're interested in
  • Any details you choose to share about your business or needs

Information collected automatically

This website does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking scripts. Your browser may transmit standard technical data to our hosting provider (Render) — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit — as part of normal web server logging. We do not use this data to identify, profile, or track you.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit through our contact form to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and follow up within 24 hours as stated
  • Understand your business needs and determine how we can help
  • Prepare quotes, proposals, or service agreements if you proceed
  • Communicate with you throughout any active service engagement

We will not use your contact information to send unsolicited marketing emails. If you become a client, we may occasionally send you relevant updates about your services (e.g., site uptime alerts, campaign reports). You can opt out of these at any time by emailing us.

4. How Your Information Is Transmitted and Stored

Contact form submissions are processed through FormSubmit.co, a third-party form handling service. When you submit the form, your data is transmitted to FormSubmit's servers and forwarded to our email inbox. FormSubmit's own privacy policy governs how they handle data in transit. We recommend reviewing it at formsubmit.co/privacy.

Once received in our email inbox, your submission is stored within our email system (Apple iCloud Mail). We retain inquiry emails for up to 3 years for business record purposes. If you become a client, relevant contact information is retained for the duration of our working relationship and for a reasonable period thereafter for legal and accounting purposes.

This website is hosted on Render (render.com). Standard server logs may be retained by Render per their own data practices. Our domain and site files do not contain a database — there is no backend storing your submissions beyond what is transmitted to our email.

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not set any first-party cookies and does not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies. The only external resources loaded are Google Fonts (for typography). Google Fonts may log your IP address as part of delivering font files — this is standard CDN behavior and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

If we ever introduce analytics or other tracking in the future, this policy will be updated and notice will be provided.

6. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • FormSubmit.co — as the form processing intermediary described in Section 4
  • Service delivery partners — if you engage us for services, we may share relevant contact details with platforms we use on your behalf (e.g., Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, hosting providers). You will be informed and, where applicable, asked to authorize this
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, we may disclose information to comply with a legal obligation or protect our rights

7. Client Data and Confidentiality

When we manage services on your behalf — including running ad campaigns, maintaining your website, or building software — we may have access to data that belongs to your business (customer lists, analytics, CRM records, etc.). This data:

  • Is used solely to perform the services you have contracted us for
  • Is never used for our own business purposes or shared with third parties except where required to perform the service
  • Remains your property at all times
  • Will be returned or securely deleted upon termination of our engagement, upon your written request

Specific data handling obligations for client engagements may be further detailed in individual service agreements or data processing addendums where applicable.

8. Your Rights

Regardless of your location, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your personal information (subject to any legal obligations we have to retain certain records)
  • Objection — object to us using your information for direct marketing at any time
  • Portability — request a copy of data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at kevlongalloway1999@icloud.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

If you are a resident of California, the EU, or another jurisdiction with specific data protection laws, additional rights may apply. We are committed to honoring applicable legal requirements. Please contact us if you have questions specific to your jurisdiction.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • All form submissions are transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
  • Access to our email systems is protected by strong passwords and multi-factor authentication
  • We limit access to personal information to only those people who need it to perform their work

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we use commercially reasonable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a minor, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.
