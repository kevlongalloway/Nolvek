Privacy

What we collect, and what happens to it.

Nolvek is a digital marketing agency based in Atlanta, Georgia. This page explains what information this website collects, who else sees it, and how to have it removed. Plain English, no boilerplate.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

What we collect

Only what you type, plus the technical minimum needed to stop the site being abused. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking on this site — no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no third-party cookies.

  • Contact form. Your name and email, and — if you fill them in — your business name, phone number, website, which service you're after, and your message.
  • Chat assistant. Whatever you type into it, which may include the same contact details if you choose to share them.
  • Technical data. Your IP address, seen by our hosting and security providers when you load the page or send a chat message.

We do not ask for, and have no use for, payment details, government identifiers, or any special-category personal data. Please don't put them into the chat.

Why we collect it

To reply to you. That's the whole purpose. We use your details to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, and follow up about the work you asked about. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Who processes it

We use a small number of third-party services to run this site. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job.

FormSubmit

formsubmit.co
Receives
Everything you submit through the contact form, or send from the chat’s confirmation card.
Why
Delivers it to our inbox. We have no server of our own to receive it.

Render

render.com
Receives
Your IP address and standard web request data.
Why
Hosts and serves this website.

Cloudflare

cloudflare.com
Receives
Your IP address, and your chat messages as they pass through.
Why
Runs the chat assistant’s connection and the Turnstile check that keeps bots off it.

AI model provider

Anthropic and/or Groq
Receives
The text of your chat conversation.
Why
Generates the assistant’s replies. Nothing is sent here unless you use the chat.

Google Fonts

fonts.google.com
Receives
Your IP address when the page loads a font.
Why
Serves the two typefaces this site uses.

The chat assistant, specifically

Your chat messages are sent to an external AI provider (Anthropic and/or Groq) so it can generate a reply. If you type your name, email or phone number into the chat, that text goes to them as part of the conversation.

We do not store chat transcripts. Conversations exist only in your browser while the window is open, and are gone when you close or refresh it. Our systems log only technical information about each request — timing, which provider was used, and roughly how much text was processed — never the content of what you wrote.

The assistant only emails us your details when you review them on a confirmation card and tap send. It cannot do it on its own.

The assistant is automated and can be wrong. Nothing it says is a quote, a contract, or a commitment on our part — prices and scope are confirmed by a person.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our email for as long as we may need them for the working relationship and our own records. Chat transcripts aren't kept at all. Our providers keep data according to their own retention policies, which we don't control.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies of its own and does no tracking. Cloudflare Turnstile — the anti-bot check on the chat — may store a short-lived value in your browser to remember that the check passed. It exists to tell humans from scripts, not to identify or follow you.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@nolvek.online and we'll action it. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA or the GDPR; we will honour those requests regardless of where you're writing from.

You can use this site without the chat. The contact form does the same job, and email always works.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change what we collect or who processes it, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Material changes to how we handle information already collected will be notified to anyone affected.
